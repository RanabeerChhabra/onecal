What it handles

The messy parts of meetings, handled.

One event, not four

The same invite in three inboxes and a calendar becomes one event, matched by invite ID, thread, join link and attendees.

Moved or cancelled

"Can we push to Friday 11?" moves it. "Need to cancel Monday" removes it for everyone, even if the invite is still in someone's calendar.

Tagged by who's in it

Every title starts with the people involved, so the team sees who is where at a glance.

[P][P+S][P+D+S]

Honest about doubt

Confirmed meetings go on. Strongly implied ones get a (?) and a reason. Vague "free next week?" threads stay off.

≥ 0.80 add0.60–0.79 (?)< 0.60 skip

Noise stays out

Newsletters, webinars, flight receipts and university timetables never reach the team calendar.

Everything links back

Each event carries the join link, the people, and a link to every email or calendar entry it came from.
