What it handles

The messy parts of meetings, handled.

One event, not four

The same invite in three inboxes and a calendar becomes a single event, matched by invite ID, thread, join link and attendees.

iCalUIDthreadzoom key

Moved? Cancelled?

"Can we push to Friday 11?" moves the event. "Need to cancel Monday" removes it, for everyone, even if the invite still sits in someone's calendar.

Tagged by who's in it

Every title starts with the people involved, so the whole team sees who is where without opening a thing.

[P][P+S][P+D+S]

Unsure? It says so

Confirmed meetings go straight on. Strongly implied ones get a (?) and a tentative mark. Vague "free next week?" threads stay off.

≥ 0.8 add0.6–0.8 (?)< 0.6 log

Noise stays out

Newsletters, webinars, flight receipts and university class timetables never make it onto the team calendar.

Everything links back

Each event carries the join link and a link to every email or calendar entry it came from, plus the line that set the time.
